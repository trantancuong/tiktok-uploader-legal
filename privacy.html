Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-24

"auto upload video" is a personal, single-user tool. It is not distributed to the public and is used only by its developer to upload their own local video files to their own TikTok account.

What data is accessed

The tool uses the TikTok Content Posting API, authorized via TikTok's OAuth login, to upload video files chosen by the developer to the developer's own TikTok account. No data about any other person is collected, stored, or shared.

Data storage

An OAuth access/refresh token is stored locally on the developer's own machine to authorize API requests. It is never transmitted anywhere other than to TikTok's official API.

Contact

This tool has no public users. Any questions can be directed to the developer directly.
